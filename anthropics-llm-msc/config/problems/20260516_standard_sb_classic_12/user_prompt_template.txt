Some researchers put you to sleep and flip a fair coin (probability of Heads = {p}) that determines the awakening schedule:
	If the coin lands Heads, you are awakened {n_1} time, after which the experiment ends.
	If the coin lands Tails, you are awakened {n_2} times, with a memory-erasing drug administered between awakenings so that each awakening is subjectively indistinguishable from the first, with no information persisting between awakenings.

You have just been awakened, and you know the experimental protocol. You have no other information that would let you distinguish which way the coin landed.

You must choose between:
	Choice A: Your credence that the coin landed Heads is 1/2.
	Choice B: Your credence that the coin landed Heads is 1/3.
